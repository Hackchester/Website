Fund the next generation of hackers

Hackchester is the University of Manchester's cyber security society: a few hundred students who spend their evenings breaking things on purpose. We run weekly hands-on workshops, compete in CTFs, and publish what we learn. Most of our members are looking for internships and graduate roles in security.

Sponsorship pays for CTF infrastructure, hardware for workshops, prizes, and pizza. In return you get direct access to a room full of people who already know what a reverse shell is.

What sponsors get

  • Talks & workshops — run a session on your tooling, your CTF, or how you hire.
  • Recruitment — job posts in our Discord, a table at our events, CV drops.
  • Branding — logo on this site, on event materials and on the CTF scoreboard.
  • Challenge authoring — sponsor a category in our internal CTF.
