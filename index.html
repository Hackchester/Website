About us

          

            We are Hackchester, the University of Manchester's cyber security society. We welcome
            students from all courses and degree levels who are passionate about protecting the digital
            world and furthering their knowledge of cyber. No prior knowledge is required to join — only a
            positive attitude and willingness to learn.
          

          

            We run weekly hands-on workshops (web, pwn, rev, crypto, forensics, OSINT, hardware…), play
            CTFs as a team, and publish writeups of what we solve. The society is
            a place for like-minded people to explore, master new skills and collaborate on projects.
          

          
questions, ideas for sessions, or want to give a talk? grab us in person, on Discord, or email .
